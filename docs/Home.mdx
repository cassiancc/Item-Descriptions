Welcome to the wiki for Item Descriptions! This mod adds a unique description tooltip to all items, alongside support for modded items through generic descriptions and resource packs.

By default, all vanilla items are supported, but if you want to add more descriptions to modded blocks, or get started with custom descriptions, this is where to be!

## For Modders
- [Adding Descriptions to Modded Blocks/Items](/en/mod/item-descriptions/docs/Modded-Blocks) covers what to do to add support for Item Descriptions to your mod's blocks and items.
- [Adding Descriptions to Modded Tags](/en/mod/item-descriptions/docs/Modded-Tags) covers what to do to add support for Item Descriptions to your mod's tags.
- [Adding Descriptions to Modded Entities](/en/mod/item-descriptions/docs/Modded-Tags) covers what to do to add support for Item Descriptions to your mod's entities.

## For Datapackers
- [Adding Descriptions to Items with Custom Model Data](/en/mod/item-descriptions/docs/Custom-Model-Data) covers what to do to add Item Descriptions to items with [Custom Model Data](https://www.planetminecraft.com/forums/communities/texturing/new-1-14-custom-item-models-tuto-578834/).
- [Adding Descriptions to Players](/en/mod/item-descriptions/docs/Player-Descriptions) covers what to do to add Item Descriptions to specific Players.
- [Adding Descriptions to Player Heads](/en/mod/item-descriptions/docs/Player-Heads) covers what to do to add Item Descriptions to specific Player Heads.
